## 1. Business Overview

Northwind Industries designs and manufactures industrial battery systems for
grid-scale energy storage. In fiscal year 2023 the company operated three
manufacturing facilities (Reno, Dresden, and Penang) and employed 4,180 people
across 11 countries.

## 4. Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of December 31, 2023, the company held $498.7M in cash and cash equivalents
and $250.0M in undrawn revolving credit. Total long-term debt was $410.0M, down
from $465.0M a year earlier. The net leverage ratio improved to 0.8x from 1.4x.

Capital expenditures were $142.0M in FY2023, of which $61.0M related to the
expansion of the Penang facility, expected to add 6 GWh of annual capacity by
the second half of FY2024.

## 5. Risk Factors

Key risks include volatility in lithium and nickel prices, concentration of
supply among a small number of cathode vendors, and exposure to changes in
renewable-energy subsidy regimes in the European Union and the United States.

A sustained 10% increase in lithium carbonate prices is estimated to reduce
gross margin by approximately 1.5 percentage points absent offsetting price
increases.

## 6. Outlook

Management guided FY2024 revenue to a range of $1,520M to $1,610M, representing
growth of 18% to 25%, and expects gross margin between 36.5% and 37.5%.
